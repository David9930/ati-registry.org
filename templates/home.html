How it works

  1. DeclareDescribe in plain words how AI was used in your book, and for any cover art, illustrations, narration or translation. You confirm your email address once; it is never published.
  2. Receive an IDAn identifier such as ATI-2026-000001-7KQ4 is assigned automatically, with a public record page.
  3. Show itPut the label and ID on your copyright page or store listing and link readers to the record.

Open by intent

  • Free for every author and publisher.
  • Public. Every declaration has its own public page and a permanent ID. Definitions and marks are CC BY 4.0, and the registry may later release its records as open data.
  • Accountable. Records carry a date and history, and anyone can report a problem.
  • Independent by intent. This is a founder-run pilot with no board seated yet. See governance.
