How it works

  1. DeclareDescribe in plain words how AI was used in your book, and for any cover art, illustrations, narration or translation. For now this needs a free GitHub account.
  2. Receive an IDAn identifier such as ATI-2026-000001 is assigned automatically, with a public record page.
  3. Show itPut the label and ID on your copyright page or store listing and link readers to the record.

Open by design

  • Free for every author and publisher.
  • Open data. Records are CC0; definitions and marks are CC BY 4.0. Every change is public in version control.
  • Accountable. Records carry a date and history, and anyone can report a problem.
  • Independent by intent. This is a founder-run pilot with no board seated yet. See governance.
