Tool terms and rights

The labels describe process. They are not statements about ownership, licensing or copyright, which depend on each tool’s terms of use (these differ by provider and by plan, and change over time) and on the law of each country. For that reason every registration asks the registrant to list the tools used, with their plan or tier, and to confirm that they have checked that the tools’ terms permit use of the outputs in the publication. The registry does not verify this and gives no legal advice.
