## Principles

- **Neutral.** No author, publisher, AI company or funder can buy a label, a classification or influence over an individual record.
- **Open.** Records are CC0. Definitions and marks are CC BY 4.0. The code and the full history of every change are public.
- **Free to register.** Registration will stay free for authors and publishers.
- **Accountable.** Declarations carry a date and a history, and anyone can challenge one.
- **Continuous.** The data can be exported and mirrored by anyone. If the registry ever stops, a complete public copy and an archived copy will be left behind.

## Intended oversight

The founder intends to hand the registry to an independent steward. The first step is an interim advisory council of five to seven people, then a formal board of a legal entity or a fiscal sponsor. The intended make-up draws on:

- the AI and technology world
- a publisher
- an established author
- the book industry and its metadata standards
- a librarian or archivist
- a self-published author
- an intellectual property lawyer
- an author who does not use AI

## Rules the board is intended to adopt

- No single category holds more than one third of the seats.
- The founder holds one seat, does not chair, and steps back after the hand-over.
- Fixed terms, recusal on conflicts of interest, and published minutes and funders.
- Funders do not get seats and have no say in classifications or individual records.
- Definitions change only through a public, versioned process.
