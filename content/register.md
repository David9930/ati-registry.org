## What you will be asked

- Your **role**: author, publisher, or an authorized representative of either. The registry does not verify this.
- The **title, author name (as it appears on the book), year**, and optionally the ISBN, edition and a link to the book&rsquo;s page.
- The **label for the text**: [Human Authored, AI Master Edited or AI Co-Authored](/labels/).
- A **plain-language statement** of how AI was, or was not, used.
- The **AI tools** used, with the provider, plan or tier, and what each was used for.
- For **cover art, illustrations, narration and translation**: whether each is human-made, AI-assisted or AI-generated.
- Four **confirmations**, including that you have checked that each tool&rsquo;s terms permit use of its output in your publication.

## What happens next

1. An automated check validates the form. If something is missing or invalid, a reply explains what to fix, and you open a new submission.
2. If it passes, an ID such as `ATI-2026-000001` is assigned, the record is saved, and the reply on your submission gives the permanent link. The record page goes live a minute or two later.
3. Accounts less than {{ cfg.min_account_age_days }} days old, or unusual volumes of submissions from one account, are held for a quick manual review.

## What is public

Everything you enter in the form, and the GitHub username that submitted it, is public and permanent, like an ISBN record. Do not include private information. Records are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0) so that libraries, retailers and researchers can reuse them freely.

## Self-declared, not verified

The registry does not check your declaration and is not a certification. Its credibility comes from the record being public, dated and open to challenge: see [how disputes work](/disputes/). A false declaration can be flagged, withdrawn or removed.

## Changing or withdrawing a record

Use the [withdrawal and report form]({{ cfg.update_url }}). The original registrant can withdraw a record automatically. A withdrawn record stays visible with the status &ldquo;withdrawn&rdquo; and its ID is never reused. Corrections to a declaration are reviewed by a maintainer and recorded in the record&rsquo;s history.

## No GitHub account?

For now a free GitHub account is required. Other ways to register are planned.
